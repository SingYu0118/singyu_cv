# Sing-Yu Chen Portfolio — Design Direction

## Concept

An animated 104-style professional profile: clear enough for recruiters, modern enough to demonstrate product taste, and detailed enough for engineering interviews.

## Narrative

1. Professional positioning and live text flip.
2. BraveTalk Club as the featured independent product.
3. Career and education timeline.
4. Rich technology overview.
5. De-identified company project outcomes.
6. Contact and protected CV download.

## Visual language

- Airy composition with large spacing and asymmetric sections.
- Soft organic bubbles instead of rectangular UI cards.
- Animated noise surface for the hero and contact sections.
- Aceternity-inspired selected-work carousel with a focused center card, dimensional side previews, numbered pagination, keyboard/swipe controls and restrained sparkles.
- Mint, sky, violet and peach gradients on warm neutral surfaces.
- No portrait or stock photography.

## Public information rules

- BraveTalk may show product architecture, model choice, features and verified outcomes.
- Company projects are de-identified and limited to public outcomes and technology capability.
- The enterprise private AI product does not expose implementation frameworks or internal architecture.
- Do not publish phone number, street address, age, CV passphrase or BraveTalk visitor passphrase.
- Do not include benchmark caveat prose on the website; show only verified result values.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop uses wide editorial grids and a dimensional project carousel with side previews.
- Tablet collapses text and feature sections to one column.
- Mobile uses large touch targets, two-column metrics and full-width carousel cards with swipe and arrow navigation.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable continuous animation.
